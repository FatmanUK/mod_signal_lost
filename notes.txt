The "Composer's Pass" Principles:

    Removing "Clutter" Notes: In your patterns, we have been triggering the lead (06) on almost every beat. Let’s remove every second note and replace them with a 1xx (Portamento up) or 2xx (Portamento down) command. This creates a "sliding" effect that sounds much more organic and less "staccato."

    Arpeggio Automation: Instead of manually inputting a chord (e.g., C-E-G), we can put a single note down and use the 0xy effect to cycle through those notes rapidly. It saves space and adds that signature "chiptune wobble."

Where to prune elsewhere:

    The Bass (04): In patterns 01 and 06, you don't need a bass note on every single beat. Try removing the bass note on Row 04 and Row 12. The kick drum will imply the rhythm, and the bass will sound "punchier" when it does hit on the 1st and 3rd beats.

    The Hi-Hats (01): Instead of a continuous 16th-note stream, try a "gallop" pattern. Keep the hats on rows 00, 04, 08, 12 (the standard pulse), but remove the ones on 02, 06, 10, 14. It immediately makes the track sound less "mechanical" and more like a live drummer.

    The Pad (05): Since this is an ambient element, you only need to trigger it once every 16 rows. Use the Cxx volume slide effect to make it swell and recede over the course of the pattern rather than having it "re-trigger" its attack phase constantly.
