Pattern 09: The Interlude Groove

This pattern creates a half-time breakdown to give the listener's ears a rest right in the middle of the track. It drops the syncopated breakbeat from earlier patterns in favor of a heavy, dragging groove. The Cymbal1 (08) crashes on the downbeats on Channel 2, the Heaven pad (05) holds a darker chord variation on Channel 3, and the Snare1 (02) hits only on beat 3 (rows 08, 18, 28, 38).
